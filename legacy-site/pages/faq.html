Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between RO and UV water purifiers?

RO (Reverse Osmosis) removes dissolved solids, chemicals and microorganisms through a semi-permeable membrane. UV (Ultraviolet) uses ultraviolet light to kill bacteria and viruses but does not remove dissolved solids or chemicals. RO provides comprehensive purification while UV is primarily for disinfection.

How often should RO membranes be replaced?

RO membranes typically need replacement every 2-3 years depending on water quality and usage. Pre-filters and post-filters should be replaced every 6-12 months. Regular AMC contracts ensure timely replacement.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, Energy System provides professional installation services for all RO systems we supply. Our certified technicians install and commission the system with full testing and user training. Installation is available across Agra and surrounding areas.

What is included in the AMC contract?

AMC contracts include quarterly preventive maintenance, filter and membrane replacement, performance reporting with TDS monitoring, and priority call-out response. Plans are available in Basic, Premium, and Enterprise tiers.

How much water does an RO system waste?

RO systems typically have a recovery rate of 25-40%, meaning 60-75% of the input water is recovered as purified water, while the remainder (concentrate/waste water) carries the removed contaminants. Modern high-recovery systems can achieve 60%+ recovery. Water can be reused for gardening and cleaning.

What is the typical lifespan of an RO system?

With proper maintenance and regular filter/membrane replacement, an RO system can last 10-15 years. The system's major components have varying lifespans: pre-filters (6-12 months), post-filters (6-12 months), membranes (2-3 years), and the pump and controller can last 5-10 years with proper care.
