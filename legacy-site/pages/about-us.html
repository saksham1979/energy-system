About Energy System

Trusted water purification partners since 2009

"Water is not one-size-fits-all."

Different homes, businesses and institutions require different purification systems. Energy System helps customers choose, install and maintain suitable water-treatment solutions.

What started as a small operation near Kargil Petrol Pump, Sikandra has grown into a trusted name in water purification systems. We deal in Domestic RO purifiers, Commercial RO plants, Industrial RO systems, Water ATM/vending machines, and genuine spare parts — with Pan-India delivery and service from our head office.
